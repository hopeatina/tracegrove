# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing anyone's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's [private reporting channel](https://github.com/hopeatina/tracegrove/security/advisories/new) and mark the report as a conduct issue, or reach out to a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly do so, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
